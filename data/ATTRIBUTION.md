## Hand-maintained inputs

Three files under `ingest/manual/` are edited by hand rather than downloaded:

- `steam_territory.geojson` — an **approximation** of the Con Edison Manhattan
  steam service area, drawn as Manhattan south of 96th St. Not authoritative.
- `uten_pilots.geojson` — thermal energy network pilot footprints. Empty until
  those boundaries are published as geodata.
- `upstate_sites.csv` — upstate facilities the Atlas does not list, each with
  its own provenance note.
