## Hand-maintained inputs

Files under `ingest/manual/` are edited by hand rather than downloaded:

- `steam_territory.geojson` — an **approximation** of the Con Edison Manhattan
  steam service area, drawn as Manhattan south of 96th St. Not authoritative.
- `uten_pilots.geojson` — thermal energy network pilot footprints. Empty until
  those boundaries are published as geodata.
- `upstate_sites.csv` — upstate facilities the Atlas does not list, each with
  its own provenance note.
- `<region>_mw.csv` — capacities taken from operator statements and public
  filings, each row carrying a source URL. Rows marked `UNVERIFIED` were seeded
  by an automated research pass and are treated as estimates until a person
  has read the source.
- `<region>_dcs.csv` — seeded data centers for regions the Atlas covers
  thinly, each with the operator page that states its location and floor area.
- `seattle_zones.geojson` — a hand-drawn **approximation** of Enwave Seattle's
  district steam service area. Not authoritative.
- `nova_zones.geojson`, `pdx_zones.geojson`, `svy_zones.geojson`,
  `la_zones.geojson`, `sac_zones.geojson` — empty. No district steam system or
  thermal energy network territory to draw.
